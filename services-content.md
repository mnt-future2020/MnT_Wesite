## Tab: AI

### AI & Intelligent Automation
AI that does real work inside your business systems, not just answers questions.

- **Enterprise AI Agents**: Agents that act inside your systems, within clear limits, logging every step.
- **AI Workflow Automation**: Repeated daily work done automatically, with a person checking where it matters.
- **Multi-Agent Systems**: Several AI agents sharing one job, each handling its own part.
- **AI Customer Support**: Common customer questions answered instantly, with hard cases passed to your team.
- **AI Sales Automation**: Leads followed up, qualified and booked in, without manual chasing.
- **AI Document Processing**: Invoices, forms and contracts read and entered, without retyping.
- **Enterprise Knowledge Management**: Your documents and policies searchable, with answers that show the source.

### Voice AI & Conversational AI
AI that talks with your customers by phone, WhatsApp and chat.

- **AI Voice Calling Agents**: AI agents that make and answer calls in a natural voice.
- **AI Contact Center Solutions**: Calls answered, routed and summarised, so your agents handle what needs them.
- **AI Receptionist**: Enquiries and bookings handled by phone, at any hour.
- **Multilingual Voice Assistants**: Voice assistants that speak with customers in their own language.
- **WhatsApp AI Automation**: Orders, enquiries and updates handled on WhatsApp automatically.
- **Conversational AI Chatbots**: Chat on your website and apps that answers from your own content.
- **SIP & PBX Integration**: Voice AI connected to the phone system you already use.

Software built around how your business actually works, not the other way round.

- **Enterprise ERP Development**: One system for finance, purchasing, stock and operations, built to your process.

- **HRMS & Payroll Systems**: Attendance, leave, payroll and employee records in one system.

- **Workflow Management Systems**: Approvals and handovers moved out of email into one tracked flow.

Software products, from the first version to a platform many customers use.

- **White-Label Solutions**: Ready software under your brand and name, customised to you.
- **MVP & Product Development**: A first version to test with real users, then the full product.
- **Enterprise Platform Engineering**: Shared foundations your teams build on: accounts, permissions, billing and APIs.
- **Legacy Software Modernization**: Old software rebuilt on modern technology without stopping the business.

### Web & Mobile Application Development
Websites and apps your customers, partners and staff use every day.

- **Enterprise Web Applications**: Browser-based software for your teams, fast and secure.
- **Android & iOS Applications**: Apps for Android and iPhone, published under your company's name.
- **Progressive Web Applications**: Websites that install and work like apps, even on weak connections.
- **B2B & B2C Platforms**: Platforms for selling to businesses, to consumers, or to both.
- **Customer & Partner Portals**: Self-service for customers and partners: orders, documents, tickets and accounts.
- **Marketplace Development**: Many sellers on one platform, with onboarding, commission and payouts handled.

### UI/UX & Digital Product Engineering
Software that people find easy to use, and old software made new.

- **Enterprise UI/UX Design**: Screens designed around how your staff and customers actually work.
- **SaaS Product Design**: Product design that helps users sign up, get started and stay.
- **Dashboard & Interface Design**: Complex data and controls made clear at a glance.
- **Product Prototyping**: Clickable prototypes to test ideas before anything is built.
- **Software Re-engineering**: Existing software restructured to be faster and easier to change.
- **Application Modernization**: Dated applications given a modern design and technology, keeping your data.

## Tab: Data & integration

### API & Enterprise System Integration
Your systems connected, so data flows between them without retyping.

- **API Development**: Clean, documented APIs that let your systems and partners connect.
- **Third-Party API Integration**: Outside services connected to your software: couriers, tax, maps and messaging.
- **ERP & CRM Integration**: Sales, finance and operations data kept in step across systems.
- **Payment Gateway Integration**: UPI, card and wallet payments, with refunds and reconciliation.
- **WhatsApp & Telephony Integration**: WhatsApp, calls and SMS connected to your CRM and support tools.
- **Legacy System Integration**: Older systems connected to new ones, without a risky replacement.
- **Enterprise Middleware Development**: The layer that moves and translates data between all your systems.

### Data Engineering & Business Intelligence
Your data collected, cleaned and turned into decisions.

- **Data Engineering**: Data from every system collected, cleaned and kept reliable.
- **Data Pipeline Development**: Automated flows that move data where it is needed, on schedule.
- **Business Intelligence Dashboards**: The numbers that matter, live on one screen.
- **AI-Powered Analytics**: Ask questions of your data in plain language and get answers.
- **Predictive Analytics**: Forecasts for sales, demand and stock based on your history.
- **Data Warehousing**: All your business data in one place, ready for reporting.
- **Reporting Automation**: Reports built and sent on their own, on time, every time.

## Tab: Cloud & security

### Cloud & DevOps Engineering
Infrastructure that stays up, scales, and costs no more than it should.

- **AWS & Cloud Solutions**: Your systems designed and run on AWS and other clouds.
- **Cloud Infrastructure Architecture**: Cloud set-up planned for uptime, security and growth from the start.
- **Cloud Migration**: Systems moved to the cloud step by step, without downtime surprises.
- **DevOps Automation**: Builds, tests and server set-up automated, so releases are routine.
- **CI/CD Implementation**: Every change tested and deployed automatically, safely and often.
- **Database Optimization**: Slow queries and databases tuned so your software stays fast.
- **Cloud Cost Optimization**: Cloud bills cut by removing waste and right-sizing what you run.

### Cybersecurity & IT Infrastructure
Your software, data and people protected, with proof to show for it.

- **Application Security**: Security built into your software, from the code to release.
- **API Security**: APIs protected with proper authentication, limits and monitoring.
- **Cloud Security**: Cloud accounts, networks and data locked down and watched.
- **Vulnerability Assessment**: Weak points found and ranked before someone else finds them.
- **Identity & Access Management**: The right people get the right access, and nobody else.
- **Security Audits**: A thorough check of your systems, with a clear list of fixes.
- **IT Infrastructure Management**: Servers, networks and devices kept updated, monitored and running.
